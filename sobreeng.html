Founded by Jeff Bezos, Blue Origin focuses on suborbital flights with the reusable New Shepard rocket.

• Altitude: Crosses the Kármán Line (100 km)
• Experience: 3 to 5 minutes of zero gravity and panoramic views of Earth

Estimated Values: Between US$ 250,000 and US$ 450,000 per seat (no public fixed price list available)

Virgin Galactic uses a different concept: a carrier aircraft that launches a rocket-powered spaceship (like VSS Unity) from the air.

• Focus: Provide an immersive space flight experience for tourists and researchers.
• Training: Passengers go through days of physical preparation before the flight.

Current Ticket Price: Recent costs set around US$ 750,000 per seat (previously sold for about US$ 450,000).

Curatorial companies and specialized travel agencies, like RocketBreaks, plan to structure complete tourism packages combining orbital hotels, flights, and exclusive space adventures.

The market aims to make off-planet luxury tourism viable in the coming decades.

Custom Costs: Packages are custom-tailored according to client preferences, with prices varying greatly depending on the chosen type of adventure (ranging from zero-gravity tours on Earth to high-end space ticket bookings).
